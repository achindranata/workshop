How to read this
  • APBN realisation is cumulative from January. Targets are the APBN 2026 posture (Pendapatan Rp3.153,6 T · Belanja Rp3.842,7 T · Defisit Rp689,1 T / 2,68% PDB · TKD Rp693,0 T).
  • Deficit as % of GDP uses the GDP implied by the APBN 2026 deficit design.
  • Converters use BI's transaction rates: the mid-rate is the average of BI's buy and sell rates.
  • Belanja K/L simulator: the ceiling is Belanja K/L in APBN 2026 (Rp1.510,5 T). Extra spending raises Belanja Negara and the deficit one-for-one, capped at 3% of GDP (UU 17/2003).
Demo notice: APBN, regional transfer and ministry figures are simulated (APBN_2026_Dummy.xlsx) and are not official Ministry of Finance data. Exchange rate, BI-Rate, inflation and reserves figures are public Bank Indonesia data as retrieved on 22 October 2026.
