01 · Shared setup for Q13 and Q14

Problem setup

Original text (Halaman 10)
Untuk Soal 13 dan 14, perhatikan sebuah anuitas hidup seumur hidup (whole life annuity) sebesar 1 dengan penangguhan (deferred) 10 tahun, yang bersifat fully discrete, pada (50). Diberikan:
(i) Mortalitas mengikuti Illustrative Life Table.
(ii) i = 0,06
(iii) Nilai sekarang dari manfaat di masa depan adalah
Z = 0,jika K50 ≤ 9; v10 äK50−9,jika K50 ≥ 10.

In English. A fully discrete whole life annuity-due of 1 per year on a life aged 50, deferred 10 years. Mortality follows the Illustrative Life Table and i = 0.06. Z is the present value of the future benefits, where K50 is the curtate future lifetime of (50).

How to read Z

  • If (50) dies before 60 (K50 ≤ 9), nothing is paid: Z = 0.
  • If (50) reaches 60, payments of 1 are made at times 10, 11, …, K50: that is K50 − 9 payments, valued at time 10 and discounted by v10.
  • Given survival to 60, K60 = K50 − 10, so K50 − 9 = K60 + 1. Hence Z = v10 · I · äK60+1, where I = 1 if (50) survives to 60 (probability 10p50).

02 · Question 13

Variance of Z

Original question
13. Hitunglah varians dari Z.
a.0,485b.3,664c.4,149d.4,634e.6,683

In English. Calculate the variance of Z.

1Write Z as a deferral factor times an annuity on (60)

Z = v10 · I · äK60+1,   I = 1 with probability 10p50

2First moment

E[Z] = v10 · 10p50 · ä60 = 10E50 · ä60

3Second moment — square the discount, but not the survival probability

E[Z2] = v20 · 10p50 · E[ äK60+12 ] = v10 · 10E50 · E[ äK60+12 ]

Because I2 = I, the survival probability appears once while the discount v10 is squared.

4Second moment of the annuity-due on (60)

Using äK+1 = (1 − vK+1) / d and E[vK+1] = A60, E[v2(K+1)] = 2A60:

E[ äK60+12 ] = 1 − 2A60 + 2A60d2  =  2A60 − (A60)2d2 + ä602

5Combine

Var(Z) = E[Z2] − (E[Z])2

02 · Question 13

Cross-check and common trap

Why the deferral matters
Z carries two sources of risk: whether (50) reaches 60, and how long (60) then lives. A correct variance must include both — the cross-check below confirms this by conditioning on I.

Cross-check by conditioning on survival to 60

Var(Z) = E[Var(Z | I)] + Var(E[Z | I]) = v20 · 10p50 · Var(äK60+1) + (v10 ä60)2 · 10p50 · 10q50

03 · Question 14

Premium by normal approximation

Original question
14. Sebuah perusahaan asuransi mengenakan premi tunggal bersih (net single premium) P untuk masing-masing dari 60 polis anuitas hidup seumur hidup dengan penangguhan 10 tahun sebesar 1 pada (50), yang bersifat fully discrete. Masa hidup dari ke-60 pemegang polis tersebut saling independen. Dengan menggunakan hampiran normal (normal approximation), carilah nilai P sedemikian sehingga peluang perusahaan asuransi mengalami kerugian adalah 20%.
a.6,0b.6,2c.6,4d.6,6e.6,8

In English. An insurer charges a premium P for each of 60 independent policies of the same 10-year deferred, fully discrete whole life annuity of 1 on (50). Using the normal approximation, find P so that the probability of a loss is 20%.

1Aggregate present value of benefits

S = Z1 + Z2 + … + Z60,   E[S] = 60 E[Z],   Var(S) = 60 Var(Z)

Variances add because the 60 lifetimes are independent. Each Zj has the distribution of Z from Q13.

2Define the loss event

Premiums collected are 60P. The insurer loses money when benefits exceed premiums:

Pr(S > 60P) = 0.20  ⇔  Pr(S ≤ 60P) = 0.80
Inputs carried from Q13
E[Z] = 5.6932  ·  Var(Z) = 6.6828

Exam reminders

  • In E[Z2], the deferral discount becomes v20 but the survival probability stays 10p50; that is why v10 · 10E50 appears.
  • Read A60 and 2A60 from the table and divide by 1000; do not rebuild them from ä60 when the table already gives them.
  • A 20% probability of loss means the premium sits at the 80th percentile of S: use z = 0.842, not 0.20 or 1.282.
  • Q14 depends on Q13: carry E[Z] and Var(Z) unrounded to keep the third decimal of P stable.
