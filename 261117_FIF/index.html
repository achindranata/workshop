          Copilot Cowork · Fabric IQ plugin

          Cowork — from a Power BI report to a finished dashboard

          Cowork Premium + Copilot Credits

          Hand it an outcome — it plans the steps, works across your apps and data, and asks before anything leaves the building.

          FIF use case · one-click demo
          Weekly collection & portfolio review

          Cowork asks the Power BI report through Fabric IQ, compares regions with last week and target, checks Teams and email for why branches slipped, then builds a self-contained HTML dashboard and drafts the email to Regional Heads.

          Good to know · Fabric IQ in Cowork
          • Generally available and installed by default in Cowork.
          • Runs as the user: Power BI permissions and RLS apply; no extra Fabric capacity needed.
          • Check key numbers: answers don't yet cite the source report.
          • Labels carry over to the files and emails Cowork creates; DLP isn't supported in Cowork yet.
