3 · Chat or Cowork?

Chat answers and you act. Cowork acts and you approve. A Cowork slide is added when the job:

  • has several steps across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint;
  • must run on a schedule or when a matching email arrives;
  • should take real actions — update a file, draft and send mail, book a meeting — each behind your approval.

Licensing. Every feature here needs a Microsoft Copilot (Premium) license. Cowork also needs usage-based billing (a Copilot Credits spending policy); without it users see Request access.

Feature to trigger
Cowork — scheduled task

Cowork plans the steps, works across Outlook, Teams and Excel for as long as the job takes, and asks before it sends. It can run up to 25 scheduled prompts.

What you get
  • A tracker workbook in OneDrive that updates itself every morning
  • Reminder emails drafted and waiting for one-click approval
  • A daily digest — and a task you can reopen to change the rules

Cowork acts only within your own permissions. Approve each send, or use Approve All when several reminders are waiting.
