SNP · Single Nucleotide Polymorphism

One letter of difference in our DNA

A SNP (pronounced “snip”) is a difference in a single DNA building block — a nucleotide: A, T, C or G — at one position in the genome. SNPs appear roughly once every 1,000 nucleotides, and most have no effect on health.

The APOE gene on chromosome 19 comes in versions called e2, e3 and e4. Carrying e4 raises the risk of late-onset Alzheimer's disease — yet not everyone with Alzheimer's carries it.

My MSc questionCan a CNN model detect the possibility of developing Alzheimer's for people who don't carry APOE4?

RAG · Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Let the model look things up before it answers

A large language model only knows what it saw in training. RAG first retrieves the most relevant passages from your own documents, adds them to the prompt, and the model generates an answer grounded in — and citing — those sources.

Agentic RAG goes a step further: the model plans the search, breaks a complex question into focused sub-queries across several sources, and can search again or call other tools before it answers.

Where ML meets GenAIRetrieval runs on ML — embeddings, vector similarity and ranking — while the LLM does the generation. RAG is one of the most common ways the two are integrated.
Sources: Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) — Microsoft Learn · Lewis et al., 2020 (arXiv). Illustration simplified; example question is fictional.

Who makes the technology

What is a Technology Principal?

A technology principal is the company that owns and builds the technology — the platform, software or cloud — and brings it to customers directly and through partners such as resellers and system integrators. Big principals, including hyperscalers, cover many layers at once.

The bridge role

What is a Solution Engineer?

The technical expert on the principal's side who turns a customer's business problem into a working solution they can believe in — before they buy, and while they adopt.

Step 1

Discover

Listen first: the business goal, current process, data, constraints and who decides.

Step 2

Design

Map the need to the right products and architecture — and be honest when it is not a fit.

Step 3

Demonstrate

Show it working: tailored demos, workshops and use-case sessions with the customer's own scenarios.

Step 4

Prove

Run a proof of concept or pilot, handle technical objections and security questions.

Step 5

Adopt

Hand over to partners and delivery teams, enable users, and help the solution reach real usage.

An SE isPart engineer, part storyteller, part consultant — technical depth plus business sense, working with account teams, partners and product groups.
An SE is notThe one who implements the whole project — delivery usually sits with system integrators, consultants or the customer's own IT.
