Copilot Fundamentals & Safe Use

Two things make Copilot safe and predictable at work: it's protected by the enterprise controls your organization already uses, and it grounds every answer in sources you choose. This module covers the fundamentals you'll lean on in every later lesson.

1.1 Secure by design — Enterprise Data Protection (EDP)

Copilot doesn't bypass your security — it rides on top of it. Here's what's protecting you and where your data goes.

What Enterprise Data Protection gives you

On by default for anyone signed in with a work account.
  • Your data isn't used to train models. Your prompts, Copilot's responses, and the data it retrieves for grounding are never used to train the foundation models.
  • It inherits your existing protections. Copilot applies your organization's Microsoft 365 identity, security, compliance and privacy policies — including Microsoft Purview and sensitivity labels.
  • It respects permissions. Copilot can only surface content you already have access to — it never widens your reach.
  • Same protection across model providers. OpenAI and Anthropic run as Microsoft subprocessors under EDP (Anthropic is outside the EU Data Boundary). Web queries go to Bing de-identified, under Bing’s own terms.

How a prompt flows

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
1 · You prompt Copilot

From Chat or inside an app.

2 · Grounding via Microsoft Graph

Retrieves only content you're allowed to see, and/or the web.

3 · Responsible AI check

Prompt and results pass automated RAI checks.

4 · LLM in the Microsoft Cloud

Processed in the service boundary — not used for training.

5 · Compliance & Purview

Responses honor your data-protection policies.

6 · Response back to you

Returned with citations to its sources.

Nothing new to configure. Copilot automatically inherits the security, compliance and privacy controls your organization already set in Microsoft 365.

1.3 Work IQ on/off — web-grounded vs. Work IQ–grounded chat

There aren't two separate chats anymore. There's one chat box with a single Work IQ toggle — flip it to control what Copilot grounds its answer in.

Work IQ off

Web-grounded Chat

Copilot answers from the web and the model’s training data, plus the files you hand it — none of your Microsoft 365 data. Prompt suggestions and the / references stay web- and file-based, so every prompt starts with you finding, attaching or pasting the material first.

  • Open-web knowledge
  • Plus any files you attach — and, in Outlook and Teams, the content you have open
  • Available to Basic & Premium
Work IQ on

Work IQ–grounded Chat

On by default. Copilot also reasons over your work across Microsoft 365 — emails, chats, meetings and files — trimmed to what you already have permission to see. The context is already there before you type.

  • Everything web-grounded does
  • Your emails, chats, meetings and files
  • Permission-trimmed — nothing you can't already open

Apa saja yang perlu saya tindak lanjuti dari email, chat Teams, dan rapat saya minggu ini? Buat tabel berisi permintaannya, siapa yang meminta, tenggat waktu, dan langkah berikutnya.

Notice the pattern. Both prompts begin with you supplying the raw material — the documents, the pasted text. Copilot is capable, but it starts from zero every time.

The prompt gets shorter, and the question gets bigger. You stop describing the material and start asking about your actual work.

It's one chat with a single Work IQ toggle — not two separate chat experiences.

Work IQ is part of Microsoft IQ — the intelligence platform that also brings Fabric IQ (Power BI and Fabric data), Foundry IQ and Web IQ. Work IQ is adding Dynamics 365 Sales, Dynamics 365 Customer Service and Power Platform data (public preview).

3.1 Cowork · continued

How it’s designed. Cowork is a prebuilt, autonomous agentic system that takes action, not just chats. Grounded in your emails, meetings, chats, files and data via Work IQ, it works through a multi-step job across Outlook, Teams, Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Where Copilot Chat answers in a single turn, Cowork works over minutes to hours — asking for your approval before sensitive actions, running in a secure, isolated environment, and acting only within your own permissions. Getting access takes two things, not one: an active Microsoft Copilot license and usage-based (Cowork) billing enabled — the spending policy is an access control, not just a budget. Without one, you’ll see Request access.

Reach for Cowork when…

  • the task is multi-step and can run largely on its own (e.g. research → draft → compile → follow-up), not a single prompt;
  • it should work over your own Microsoft 365 data (Work IQ) and take real actions — send mail, post in Teams, build docs, manage files — each behind an approval;
  • you want a ready-made agent you can extend with up to 50 custom skills and Microsoft 365 App Store plugins & connectors, rather than building one in Agent Builder or Copilot Studio;
  • it should run on a schedule — recurring prompts such as a daily briefing or a follow-up sweep;
  • the work stays within one person’s identity and permissions, with you reviewing the result.
Where Cowork runs, how approvals work, and what else it can do
Where it runsYour browser at copilot.cloud.microsoft, the Copilot desktop app for Windows and macOS, the Copilot mobile app, and Outlook and Teams. You need a Microsoft Copilot license and a spending policy — without one, you’ll see Request access.
ApprovalsCowork asks before sensitive actions. Approve once, use Approve All when several are waiting, or skip similar prompts for the rest of the session. Review or revoke these choices under Permissions in the side panel; automated tasks can be pre-authorized when you create them.
What else it can doEdit an existing Word, Excel or PowerPoint file in OneDrive or SharePoint · start a task when a matching email or Teams message arrives · complete web tasks in Microsoft Edge on your device · generate images · run up to 25 scheduled prompts. Leave the model on Auto; reasoning effort defaults to Medium.

Coming to Chat (Frontier). Copilot Chat can also run long-running, multi-step work for users with a Microsoft Copilot license — track it in the Tasks view at the top right of the navigation pane; sensitive actions still need your approval. Cowork stays the choice for end-to-end work you delegate.
